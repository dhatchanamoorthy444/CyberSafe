Why This Works

CyberSafe does not visit the URL. It analyzes the structure of the URL itself — its hostname, path, query parameters, and scheme — to detect deception patterns.

SAFE does not guarantee the destination is safe. It means no significant structural indicators were detected. A real malicious site hosted at a legitimate domain will not be flagged by structural analysis alone.
